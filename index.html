ABOUT CTI

Built in Asia.
Designed for connected finance.

Founded in 1999, CTI Group has evolved from technology and human-resource operations into a cross-border FinTech partner. Since 2015, the group has expanded its strategic investment across the Greater Bay Area, building offshore development, FinTech support and cross-border operations capabilities.

Today, multidisciplinary teams support financial, public-service and enterprise clients through infrastructure, applications, automation, data and AI.
