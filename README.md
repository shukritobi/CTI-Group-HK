# CTI Group HK — Website Redesign Concept

Independent UI/UX redesign concept for **CTI Group Limited (博賢集團)**, prepared as a proactive application piece for the company's UI/UX Designer opening in Kuala Lumpur.

## Concept direction

The current CTI website contains strong business information but presents much of it as long-form corporate copy. This concept reorganizes that story around:

- Clearer positioning for CTI as a cross-border FinTech and IT operations group
- Strong visual hierarchy and responsive/mobile-first presentation
- Faster discovery of the six core business units
- A bilingual English / Traditional Chinese interaction
- More contemporary AI, cloud and digital-operations visual language
- Easier scanning of CTI's operational solutions and regional network
- Clear disclosure that this is an independent redesign concept, not CTI's official site

## Public information used

Company information was researched from CTI Group's public web presence, including:

- CTI Group Limited official website: https://www.ctigroup.hk/
- CTI Group LinkedIn company page
- Public UI/UX Designer job description posted by CTI Group

The concept references CTI's publicly described six business areas: ITSM, AOC, ODC, FAC, FOC and GAC, and its offices in Macau, Hong Kong, Zhuhai, Taipei and Auckland.

## Stack

Static HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript. No framework or build step is required. The site includes responsive layouts, reduced-motion support, interaction states, scroll reveal and bilingual copy switching.
